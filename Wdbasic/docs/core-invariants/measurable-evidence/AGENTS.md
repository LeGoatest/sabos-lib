# Measurable Evidence Agent Instructions

> **Status:** Binding under `core-invariants/measurable-evidence/`  
> **Parent:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)

Never upgrade guidance into a standard, a practitioner position into a universal law, or a heuristic into empirical proof. Preserve exact publication status, version, scope, test state, and unresolved evidence.

Historical research should remain available even when later WDBASIC revisions reject or supersede its conclusions.
