# Core Invariants Agent Instructions

> **Status:** Binding under `core-invariants/`  
> **Parent:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)

Core-invariant changes are normative changes. Do not weaken an invariant to fit a current implementation.

Before editing, identify the owning invariant subdomain and read its local `README.md`/`AGENTS.md` where present.

Do not introduce technology-specific requirements into the core unless the requirement is truly technology-independent. Do not convert experience metrics, content heuristics, practitioner preferences, or research findings into core law without explicit governance change.
