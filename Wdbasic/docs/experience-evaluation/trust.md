# Trust Evaluation

Evaluate whether users have a factual basis for believing material claims and understanding who is accountable.

Review authentic reviews/testimonials, real project evidence, business or author identity, service scope and limitations, relevant qualifications, transparent process/policies/pricing context, response expectations, and consistency between claim, proof, and action.

Missing proof remains missing. Trust evaluation may never reward fabricated or implied evidence prohibited by the truthful-content invariant.
