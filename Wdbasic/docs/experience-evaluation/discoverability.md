# Discoverability Evaluation

Evaluate whether intended users and relevant discovery systems can find, interpret, and reach the content.

Review as applicable:

- crawlable/internal navigation structure;
- indexability, canonical behavior, redirects, and missing-route status integrity;
- page titles, headings, metadata, structured-data locations, and descriptive media context;
- unique useful subject coverage instead of thin or doorway-like duplication;
- natural audience/search terminology instead of phrase repetition;
- coherent service/location/topic relationships and internal linking.

Do not treat keyword density as a quality target. Rendering strategy belongs to the active technology profile; JavaScript-free content is not represented as a universal Google requirement.
