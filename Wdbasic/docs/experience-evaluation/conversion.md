# Conversion Evaluation

Evaluate whether an appropriate next action is clear without manipulation.

Review the clarity of the primary action, action wording, CTA timing/intensity relative to user readiness, form friction, objection/evidence placement, response expectations, alternate contact paths, and absence of fake urgency, deceptive defaults, forced consent, or manufactured scarcity.

High-intent visitors may appropriately receive an immediate transaction/contact CTA. Early-research visitors may need comparison, examples, process, or lower-commitment actions first.
