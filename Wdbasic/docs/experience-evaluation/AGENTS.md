# Experience Evaluation Agent Instructions

> **Status:** Binding maintenance rules for diagnostic evaluation  
> **Parent:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)

Evaluate dimensions independently and report evidence and unknowns. Do not average away a weak dimension and do not use an experience score to override a core-invariant failure.

Metrics must identify their source, scope, date/version where material, and whether they are field data, lab data, qualitative evidence, or practitioner judgment.
