# WDBASIC Glossary Agent Instructions

> **Status:** Binding for work under `Wdbasic/docs/glossaries/`  
> **Parent authority:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)  
> **Glossary entry point:** [`README.md`](README.md)

Glossaries clarify WDBASIC terminology. They are informative unless a binding contract explicitly adopts a glossary definition.

## Rules

Agents MUST:

- preserve distinctions between formal standard terminology, security terminology, WDBASIC-defined terms, and ordinary industry shorthand;
- cite or identify the owning standard/provider when a term has an external authoritative definition;
- record ambiguity when the same term is used differently across disciplines;
- avoid turning a glossary explanation into a new binding requirement by implication.

Agents MUST NOT rewrite normative contracts merely to make them match a preferred glossary wording.

Critical acronyms and requirements should still be understandable in binding documents without requiring a glossary lookup.
