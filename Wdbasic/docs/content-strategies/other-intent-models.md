# Other Intent Models

WDBASIC explicitly permits content architectures outside PAS, comparison, informational, and transactional structures.

Examples include:

- navigational/support;
- onboarding/setup;
- dashboard/task-oriented;
- portfolio/evidence-first;
- aspirational/inspiration;
- retention/account management;
- tool/calculator/interactive utility;
- emergency/status communication;
- documentation/reference.

For any other model, document the user's primary intent, semantic jobs, evidence needs, action model, and why the selected sequence is better than forcing one of the named strategies.
