# Informational Strategy

Use informational structure when the primary user goal is understanding, learning, diagnosing, completing a task, or answering a question.

Prefer:

```text
direct answer / task outcome
→ explanation
→ relevant detail
→ evidence or examples
→ related questions
→ optional next step
```

Do not delay the answer merely to manufacture a persuasion sequence. Conversion may be secondary or absent.
