# SEObasic Local Search

> **Scope:** Local-intent search, Google Business Profile, Google local/map-pack visibility, business identity consistency, reviews, citations, service areas, local proof, and website/local-surface relationships.

Local search is treated as a connected system rather than a single Google Business Profile optimization checklist.

## Subject map

This domain can preserve knowledge about:

- Google Business Profile ownership, categories, services, products, descriptions, photos, posts, questions/answers, hours, and attributes;
- local/map-pack visibility and proximity/relevance/prominence considerations;
- business name, legal entity, DBA, public brand, website identity, and real-world representation;
- address versus service-area business models;
- service-area selection and location relevance;
- reviews, responses, reputation, and truthful review acquisition;
- citations and business-data consistency;
- local landing/service-area pages and first-party proof;
- local links, partnerships, community relevance, and entity relationships;
- calls, directions, website visits, bookings, forms, and other local conversion signals;
- local rank tracking and diagnostic limitations;
- platform suspensions, verification, edits, and policy risk.

## Principle

A profile, citation, review, website page, or local link is evidence only within its real context. SEObasic MUST NOT invent addresses, locations served, reviews, awards, business history, or customer proof to create stronger local signals.

See [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md) before automated changes.
