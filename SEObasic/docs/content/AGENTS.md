# SEObasic Content Agent Instructions

> **Scope:** `SEObasic/docs/content/`  
> **Parent authority:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)

## Preserve

Agents MUST preserve the distinction between:

- canonical philosophy;
- application guidance;
- platform/channel examples;
- historical records;
- research or industry evidence.

The canonical T.E.S.T.I.N.G. wording in [`testing-philosophy.md`](testing-philosophy.md) is user-authored authority and MUST NOT be silently edited, normalized, paraphrased as normative text, or replaced.

## Apply, do not redefine

When adapting content strategy to a company, website, social platform, paid campaign, Google Business Profile, local search surface, or YouTube channel:

- adapt examples and operating tactics;
- preserve the philosophy's definitions;
- distinguish durable first-party content from channel distribution;
- preserve truth, permission, attribution, and evidence requirements;
- avoid treating engagement metrics as the sole definition of useful content.

## Cross-channel work

A piece of source material may support several outputs, but agents MUST account for channel differences rather than mechanically duplicating content.

When a task primarily concerns a channel's mechanics, also read that channel's `AGENTS.md`.

## Historical material

Historical examples belong under [`../references/`](../references/README.md). Preserve what was actually recovered; do not invent missing details to make a historical narrative complete.

## Changelog

Material changes to this content framework require an entry in [`../../CHANGELOG.md`](../../CHANGELOG.md).
