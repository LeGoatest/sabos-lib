# SEObasic Content

> **Scope:** Content philosophy, content strategy, editorial/community practice, content gathering, reuse, and cross-channel source material.

This domain preserves the reasoning behind what should be created and shared, not merely a calendar of posts or a list of keywords.

Content knowledge in SEObasic can include:

- canonical practitioner philosophies;
- durable website content;
- social-media posts and community conversations;
- video/source-material planning;
- project documentation and proof;
- case studies, galleries, FAQs, and knowledge-base material;
- content reuse across owned, organic, paid, local, and video channels;
- content collection as part of normal operations rather than an afterthought.

## Channel relationship

This directory owns content philosophy and reusable content strategy. Channel-specific mechanics belong in their own domains:

- websites → [`../websites/`](../websites/README.md)
- organic social → [`../social-media/`](../social-media/README.md)
- paid/PPC → [`../paid-media/`](../paid-media/README.md)
- local/Google Business Profile/maps → [`../local-search/`](../local-search/README.md)
- YouTube → [`../youtube/`](../youtube/README.md)

A content idea may travel across several channels without the channels becoming interchangeable.
