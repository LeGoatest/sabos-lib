# SEObasic Research

> **Scope:** Scholarly research, empirical studies, experiments, measurement methodology, evidence synthesis, and limitations relevant to search, local visibility, content, paid media, social media, websites, entities, and YouTube.

Research in SEObasic exists to test, challenge, refine, or support documented positions. It does not automatically become a contract.

## Evidence model

Research records SHOULD preserve:

- research question;
- source/citation;
- study or dataset scope;
- methodology;
- relevant finding;
- limitations/confounders;
- date and platform context;
- relationship to existing SEObasic positions or contracts.

## Research categories

Likely areas include:

- search-engine behavior and information retrieval;
- local-search studies;
- content quality and user behavior;
- conversion and advertising measurement;
- social-media distribution and engagement;
- YouTube discovery/retention;
- link/entity graphs;
- documentation, accessibility, and web-performance effects where they intersect discovery/conversion.

## Rule

A correlation study does not prove causation. A vendor experiment does not automatically generalize across industries, geographies, or platforms. A practitioner result remains valuable evidence but must be labeled as such.
