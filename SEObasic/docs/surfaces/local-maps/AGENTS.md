# SEObasic Local Search Agent Instructions

> **Scope:** `SEObasic/docs/local-search/`  
> **Parent authority:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)

## Mission

Preserve real-world business truth while improving local discoverability and conversion across Google Business Profile, local results, map-pack surfaces, and the supporting website/entity system.

## Required discipline

Agents MUST:

- verify current platform guidance when a recommendation depends on changing Google Business Profile or local-search behavior;
- distinguish observed ranking correlation from documented platform behavior and from guaranteed causation;
- preserve the distinction between legal entity, DBA, public-facing brand, address, service area, and website identity;
- keep reviews, locations, service areas, hours, categories, and business attributes truthful;
- evaluate website/local-profile consistency without forcing artificial uniformity where platform fields have different purposes;
- treat suspension/verification/policy risk as materially different from ordinary optimization.

Agents MUST NOT:

- fabricate reviews, locations, offices, service areas, categories, credentials, or local project evidence;
- recommend keyword-stuffed business names that misrepresent the real-world business;
- guarantee map-pack placement;
- assume proximity can be overcome by on-page optimization alone;
- copy general organic-search guidance into GBP/local surfaces without checking platform-specific behavior.

## Contracts

Reusable local-search obligations SHOULD be formalized under this domain's future `contracts/` directory, especially for business-identity truth, review integrity, service-area representation, and website/profile consistency.
