# SEObasic Documentation

> **Status:** Canonical SEObasic knowledge index  
> **Framework root:** [`../README.md`](../README.md)  
> **Agent instructions:** [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md)

`SEObasic/docs/` contains the governed body of search, answer/generative discovery, content, channel, measurement, research, standards, reference, terminology, position, and contract knowledge for SEObasic.

SEObasic's root is navigational. Illustrative code/cases remain under [`../examples/`](../examples/), while long-form knowledge lives here.

## Domains

- `contracts/` — cross-domain binding obligations.
- `positions/` — explicit practitioner preferences and divergence.
- `content/` — content philosophy and methods, including canonical T.E.S.T.I.N.G.
- `websites/` — owned website/discovery surface and on-page search language.
- `discovery/` — Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), answer/generative discovery strategy and evidence routing.
- `technical/` — technical SEO, metadata, structured data, crawling/indexing and AI/search discovery controls.
- `entities/` — entity relationships and internal linking.
- `local-search/` — Google Business Profile, Maps/local pack, local intent, reviews, citations, and local proof.
- `social-media/` — organic social content/community practice.
- `paid-media/` — PPC, paid search/social, targeting, creative, attribution, and experiments.
- `youtube/` — YouTube discovery, packaging, retention, series, Shorts/long form, community, and analytics.
- `measurement/` — metric semantics, reporting, answer/generative citation measurement, comparability, geographic measurement, and analytics vocabulary.
- `research/` — research evidence and synthesis, including AEO/GEO and generative-search literature.
- `standards/` — formal standards, platform documentation/policy, and applicability records, including AI-search publisher guidance.
- `references/` — historical/source evidence and preserved source excerpts.
- `glossaries/` — terminology and disambiguation.

## AEO/GEO routing

Start with [`discovery/README.md`](discovery/README.md), then route by evidence type:

- strategy/terminology → [`discovery/`](discovery/README.md)
- crawlers/robots/index controls → [`technical/ai-discovery-controls.md`](technical/ai-discovery-controls.md)
- citations/answer visibility/referrals → [`measurement/ai-discovery.md`](measurement/ai-discovery.md)
- current platform behavior → [`standards/ai-search-platform-guidance.md`](standards/ai-search-platform-guidance.md)
- academic evidence/benchmarks/Google Scholar discovery → [`research/answer-generative-discovery.md`](research/answer-generative-discovery.md)

This separation prevents a research finding, vendor claim, crawler rule or platform metric from silently becoming a universal GEO requirement.

## Artifact boundary

[`../examples/`](../examples/) is intentionally outside `docs/` because examples are illustrative artifacts, not knowledge authority by existence alone.

Operational `playbooks/` may be added at the SEObasic root when real reusable execution playbooks exist. Do not create empty playbook bureaucracy merely for symmetry.

## Governing principle

> **Docs explain what is known, believed, defined, or required. Examples illustrate. Future playbooks operationalize adopted knowledge without redefining it.**
