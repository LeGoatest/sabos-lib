# SEObasic Websites

> **Scope:** First-party websites, landing pages, service pages, location pages, case studies, knowledge content, conversion/search relationships, and website-owned durable content.

Websites are the primary owned surface where a business or project can control information architecture, content depth, internal links, structured data, conversion paths, and durable proof.

This domain is intentionally broader than technical SEO. It can preserve knowledge about:

- site positioning and service focus;
- information architecture and navigation;
- service and location page strategy;
- landing pages and campaign destinations;
- case studies, galleries, FAQs, and proof;
- calls to action and conversion paths;
- relationship between website content and Google Business Profile, local results, social media, paid campaigns, and YouTube;
- first-party canonical content versus distributed channel versions;
- practitioner lessons about what website structures work or fail in real projects.
