# SEObasic YouTube

> **Scope:** YouTube channel strategy, video discovery, packaging, search, retention, series/playlists, audience relationships, and cross-channel distribution.

YouTube is treated as both a search/discovery platform and a durable content destination. Its mechanics differ from websites, short-form social feeds, and paid media even when the same source material supports all of them.

## Subject map

This domain can preserve knowledge about:

- channel positioning and audience promise;
- video topics, search/discovery intent, and content gaps;
- titles, thumbnails, descriptions, chapters, captions, and metadata;
- hooks, structure, retention, and viewer satisfaction;
- playlists, series, end screens, cards, and internal channel pathways;
- Shorts versus long-form relationships;
- comments/community interaction;
- content reuse from projects, FAQs, case studies, and social material;
- YouTube-to-website and website-to-YouTube relationships;
- sponsorship/disclosure and attribution;
- analytics interpretation including impressions, click-through rate, watch time, retention, returning viewers, and conversions;
- experiments and practitioner lessons about packaging versus content quality.

## Relationship to T.E.S.T.I.N.G.

T.E.S.T.I.N.G. may guide what stories, experiments, community interactions, and source material are gathered and shared. YouTube-specific packaging/discovery mechanics do not redefine that philosophy.
