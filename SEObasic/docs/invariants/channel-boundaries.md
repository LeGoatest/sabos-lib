# Channel Boundaries Contract

> **Status:** Binding  
> **Scope:** Websites, organic social media, paid media/PPC, local search/Google Business Profile/maps, and YouTube  
> **Owner:** SEObasic cross-domain governance

## Requirement

SEObasic channels MAY share strategy, source material, entities, offers, proof, and conversion objectives, but channel-specific behavior MUST remain governed by the mechanics, evidence, policies, and audience context of that channel.

Agents MUST NOT copy tactics or platform assumptions from one channel into another without checking applicability.

## Channel roles

At a high level:

- **Website** — owned, durable information and conversion surface with controllable architecture and canonical first-party content.
- **Organic social media** — relationship/distribution surface with platform-specific publishing and community mechanics.
- **Paid media/PPC** — purchased distribution/acquisition surface whose success requires campaign and business-outcome measurement.
- **Local search / Google Business Profile / maps** — local-intent discovery and business-identity surface governed by real-world representation and platform eligibility/policy.
- **YouTube** — durable video discovery/content surface with search, browse/recommendation, retention, and packaging mechanics.

These roles are defaults for reasoning, not claims that every project must use every channel or that a channel has only one purpose.

## Rationale

A common failure in multi-channel marketing is assuming that because content or an idea is reusable, the implementation mechanics are also reusable. This creates poor platform fit, misleading measurement, and drift in business identity or conversion intent.

## Validation

For cross-channel work, review:

1. What source material/claim is shared?
2. What channel-specific adaptation is required?
3. What platform-specific policy or behavior applies?
4. What conversion or audience objective applies to that channel?
5. What should remain canonical on an owned first-party surface?
6. What metrics actually establish success for the stated objective?

## Exceptions

None defined for silent cross-channel assumption. A deliberate cross-channel standard may be adopted when evidence shows the behavior is genuinely shared and the contract states the applicable scope.
