# Truth and Evidence Contract

> **Status:** Binding  
> **Scope:** All SEObasic domains, channels, implementations, campaigns, examples, and automated tooling  
> **Owner:** SEObasic cross-domain governance

## Requirement

SEObasic outputs MUST represent services, locations, people, organizations, reviews, results, credentials, relationships, performance, platform status, dates, and other material claims truthfully.

Agents and implementations MUST NOT fabricate or silently strengthen evidence to make content, structured data, local listings, campaigns, reports, examples, or recommendations appear more authoritative or successful.

When a claim depends on evidence, the evidence type SHOULD remain distinguishable when that distinction affects interpretation.

## Rationale

Search visibility, local presence, social/community trust, paid campaign decisions, and YouTube/content strategy become unreliable when the underlying claims are invented or when weak evidence is presented as certainty.

SEObasic therefore treats truthfulness as a cross-channel invariant rather than a platform-specific optimization tactic.

## Evidence basis

This contract consolidates existing SEObasic practitioner positions and integrity rules across content, technical structured data, local/service pages, entity relationships, historical T.E.S.T.I.N.G. applications, and repository anti-hallucination governance.

External platform policies and standards may impose additional requirements within their own scope.

## Validation

Depending on the claim, validation may include:

- checking the canonical business/profile/source record;
- checking visible page content against structured data;
- verifying real service/location eligibility;
- verifying source permission/attribution;
- verifying analytics/report definitions;
- distinguishing measured results from projections, examples, or benchmarks.

A claim that cannot be verified SHOULD be omitted, qualified, or explicitly labeled as uncertain rather than invented.

## Exceptions

Clearly labeled hypothetical examples may use invented illustrative data when no reasonable reader could mistake it for an actual project/business result.

Examples MUST NOT be represented as measured real-world evidence.
