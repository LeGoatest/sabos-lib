# SEObasic Evidence

> **Role:** Why we believe something  
> **Parent:** [`../README.md`](../README.md)  
> **Agent instructions:** [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md)

Evidence preserves the provenance, scope, maturity, and limitations behind SEObasic claims. It does not become a binding requirement merely because it exists.

## Current evidence families

- [`research/`](research/README.md) — peer-reviewed research, preprints, benchmarks/datasets, empirical studies, and research synthesis.
- [`platform-guidance/`](platform-guidance/README.md) — current platform-owner documentation, policies, formal/protocol references, and applicability records.
- [`practitioner-positions/`](practitioner-positions/README.md) — explicit adopted preferences, judgments, tradeoffs, and acknowledged practitioner bias.
- [`historical-references/`](historical-references/README.md) — preserved source excerpts and historical/non-normative evidence.

Terminology is intentionally separate under [`../terminology/`](../terminology/README.md): defining a word is not evidence for a behavioral claim.

## Claim-level authority

The binding [`Evidence Classification Contract`](../invariants/evidence-classification.md) governs how material claims preserve:

- evidence class;
- consumer/platform/surface;
- purpose;
- primary source;
- publication status;
- method/sample/domain limitations;
- review/freshness context;
- observation versus inference;
- generalization limits.

## Platform guidance versus standards

The current `platform-guidance/` tree contains the existing platform/protocol material because that is the substantive body we actually have. A separate formal-standards subdomain should be created only when enough standalone standards material exists to justify it.

Do not call a platform recommendation a formal standard merely because both are stored in the evidence layer.

## Promotion boundary

Evidence may justify:

- revising an evaluation;
- choosing or rejecting a strategy;
- updating a surface profile;
- changing a measurement interpretation;
- proposing an invariant.

It does not become any of those automatically.

> **Evidence informs authority; it does not silently manufacture authority.**
