# SEObasic Practitioner Position Agent Instructions

> **Status:** Binding for work under `SEObasic/docs/evidence/practitioner-positions/`  
> **Parent authority:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)

Agents MUST preserve the distinction between practitioner position, canonical philosophy, invariant/contract, platform/vendor guidance, formal standard, research evidence, industry convention, and historical reference.

A position record SHOULD identify:

- stance;
- practical basis;
- relevant evidence/standards/platform guidance;
- known tradeoffs;
- deliberate divergence from common practice;
- confidence/uncertainty when material.

Do not erase or normalize an explicit SEObasic position because another SEO tool, vendor, or practitioner uses a different convention.

Do not present a practitioner position as a search-engine guarantee, universal ranking rule, or cross-domain invariant merely because SEObasic has adopted it operationally.

When a practitioner position is deliberately promoted into binding behavior, make that change explicitly in the owning invariant, measurement contract, strategy, or surface authority rather than changing the evidence record silently.

Material position changes update [`../../../CHANGELOG.md`](../../../CHANGELOG.md) and may require change control when normative behavior changes.
