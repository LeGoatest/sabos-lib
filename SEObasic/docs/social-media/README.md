# SEObasic Social Media

> **Scope:** Organic social-media marketing, posts, community interaction, platform-specific publishing, content reuse, audience relationships, and social-to-owned-channel pathways.

Social media in SEObasic is not reduced to posting frequency or vanity metrics. It is a distribution and relationship surface where content, community, trust, discovery, and conversion interact.

## Subject map

This domain can preserve knowledge about:

- platform-specific content formats and constraints;
- post, carousel, story, short-video, live, poll, question, and collaboration formats;
- content calendars and sustainable publishing rhythms;
- audience engagement and community interaction;
- content repurposing from projects, websites, YouTube, and customer questions;
- attribution, permissions, disclosures, and truthful claims;
- social profile identity and link strategy;
- social-to-website, social-to-phone, social-to-message, or social-to-video conversion paths;
- platform analytics and metric interpretation;
- organic reach versus paid distribution;
- practitioner lessons about content that creates useful attention versus empty engagement.

## Relationship to T.E.S.T.I.N.G.

The T.E.S.T.I.N.G. philosophy in [`../content/testing-philosophy.md`](../content/testing-philosophy.md) is a cross-channel content/community philosophy and has historically been applied to social campaigns.

Social-media tactics may specialize how the philosophy is expressed without redefining it.
