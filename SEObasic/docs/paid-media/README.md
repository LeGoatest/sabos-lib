# SEObasic Paid Media

> **Scope:** PPC and paid campaigns across search and social platforms, campaign structure, targeting, creative, landing-page relationships, conversion measurement, budget efficiency, and paid-channel evidence.

Paid media is treated as a measurable acquisition system, not merely as boosted organic content.

## Subject map

This domain can preserve knowledge about:

- paid search and paid social campaign structure;
- campaign, ad group/ad set, audience, keyword, placement, and creative relationships;
- search intent and negative-keyword strategy;
- match types and query review;
- geographic targeting and service-area alignment;
- ad copy, creative, offers, and policy constraints;
- landing-page message match and conversion paths;
- calls, forms, purchases, bookings, messages, and offline conversions;
- attribution, tracking, conversion quality, and lead quality;
- budget, bidding, pacing, experiments, and marginal returns;
- brand versus non-brand campaigns;
- retargeting and audience exclusions;
- paid campaign reporting and the difference between platform metrics and business outcomes.

## Principle

A campaign is not successful merely because the platform reports clicks, impressions, reach, or conversions. Measurement must remain connected to the actual business objective and the quality of resulting actions.
