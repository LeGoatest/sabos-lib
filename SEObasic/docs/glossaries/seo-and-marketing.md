# SEObasic SEO and Marketing Glossary

> **Status:** Informative

## Canonical URL
The preferred URL representing a resource when multiple URLs could expose substantially the same content. Canonicalization is a signal/contract, not a guarantee that a search engine will select the declared URL.

## Citation
In local-search practice, a mention/listing of a business's identifying information on another site or directory. Citation quality and consistency are distinct from a scholarly citation.

## Conversion
A defined action that matters to the business or campaign, such as a qualified call, form submission, booking, purchase, message, or other explicitly chosen outcome. Platform-reported conversions may require independent interpretation.

## Entity
A distinct concept, person, organization, service, place, project, product, or other identifiable subject represented consistently across content or structured relationships.

## Google Business Profile (GBP)
Google's business-profile product used to represent eligible businesses across Google surfaces such as Search and Maps. Platform capabilities and policies change and should be verified against current official guidance.

## Internal link
A crawlable link from one page or resource on a site to another resource within the same governed site/domain context. SEObasic treats useful internal links as semantic/navigation relationships, not keyword insertion opportunities.

## Local pack / map pack
A common industry term for the local business result group shown for some locally oriented Google searches. Exact presentation can vary by query, device, geography, and product changes.

## Local search
Search/discovery activity where location, proximity, service area, or local business relevance materially affects the result or user intent.

## Organic social media
Unpaid publishing and community interaction on social platforms. This is distinct from paid social advertising even when the same account, creative, or content source is involved.

## PPC
Pay-per-click, commonly used as shorthand for paid digital advertising where clicks are a billing or optimization unit. In practice, paid-media platforms support multiple bidding/billing models, so PPC should not be assumed to describe every paid campaign exactly.

## Practitioner position
An explicitly documented approach adopted from accumulated practical experience or project judgment. It may agree or disagree with common industry practice and should be labeled rather than disguised as a formal standard.

## Structured data
Machine-readable data describing entities/content and their relationships, such as Schema.org data serialized as JSON-LD. It must remain consistent with truthful visible content.

## T.E.S.T.I.N.G.
The canonical SEObasic content/community philosophy defined in [`../content/testing-philosophy.md`](../content/testing-philosophy.md). Its letters and meanings must not be inferred from this glossary or replaced with another expansion.

## Vanity metric
A metric that may show activity or attention but does not by itself establish useful business or audience outcomes—for example raw impressions, follower counts, or clicks when the actual goal is qualified leads, sales, trust, retention, or another result.
