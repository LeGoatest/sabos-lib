# SEObasic SEO and Marketing Glossary

> **Status:** Informative

## AEO — Answer Engine Optimization
A scoped industry term for work intended to improve how a source is discovered, understood, selected, represented, or cited in answer-oriented experiences. AEO is not a universal web standard or a single cross-platform ranking system. See [`../discovery/answer-engine-optimization.md`](../discovery/answer-engine-optimization.md).

## AI search
A broad industry term for search/discovery experiences that use AI techniques in retrieval, ranking, synthesis, query expansion, answer generation, or interaction. The term does not identify one platform architecture or measurement model by itself.

## Answer engine
A system or search surface that directly returns or synthesizes an answer to an information need rather than only returning a ranked list of links. Implementations may use retrieval, knowledge graphs, language models, search indexes, structured data, or other techniques.

## Canonical URL
The preferred URL representing a resource when multiple URLs could expose substantially the same content. Canonicalization is a signal/contract, not a guarantee that a search engine will select the declared URL.

## Citation
An overloaded term whose meaning MUST be resolved by context.

- **Local-search citation:** a mention/listing of a business's identifying information on another site or directory.
- **Generative/answer citation:** a visible source reference or link associated with an answer generated or synthesized by an answer/generative system.
- **Scholarly citation:** a bibliographic reference to research or another published source.

These concepts are not interchangeable. A generative citation does not by itself prove authority, factual support, ranking position, or local citation consistency.

## Conversion
A defined action that matters to the business or campaign, such as a qualified call, form submission, booking, purchase, message, or other explicitly chosen outcome. Platform-reported conversions may require independent interpretation.

## Entity
A distinct concept, person, organization, service, place, project, product, or other identifiable subject represented consistently across content or structured relationships.

## GEO — Generative Engine Optimization
A research/industry term for optimizing content visibility, source selection, citation, representation, or influence in generative-engine responses. The term was formalized in the KDD 2024 paper *GEO: Generative Engine Optimization*. GEO does not imply that Google, ChatGPT, Bing/Copilot, Perplexity, or other systems share a universal ranking formula. See [`../discovery/generative-engine-optimization.md`](../discovery/generative-engine-optimization.md).

## Generative engine
A system that uses generative models to synthesize responses from retrieved, indexed, parametric, structured, or other information sources. The exact retrieval/citation architecture is platform-specific.

## Generative search
Search/discovery in which a system synthesizes an answer or response, often with retrieved web sources and citations/links, instead of presenting only conventional ranked results. Generative search is a product/system category rather than one universal algorithm.

## Google Business Profile (GBP)
Google's business-profile product used to represent eligible businesses across Google surfaces such as Search and Maps. Platform capabilities and policies change and should be verified against current official guidance.

## Grounding
The use of retrieved or supplied external information to support a generated response. Platforms may use the term differently. Grounding does not guarantee that every answer claim is fully supported or visibly cited.

## Grounding query
A platform/research term for a query generated or used during retrieval to gather supporting information for a generative response. When reporting a platform-owned grounding-query metric, preserve the platform's exact definition. Bing Webmaster Tools currently uses this term in AI Performance reporting.

## Internal link
A crawlable link from one page or resource on a site to another resource within the same governed site/domain context. SEObasic treats useful internal links as semantic/navigation relationships, not keyword insertion opportunities.

## Local pack / map pack
A common industry term for the local business result group shown for some locally oriented Google searches. Exact presentation can vary by query, device, geography, and product changes.

## Local search
Search/discovery activity where location, proximity, service area, or local business relevance materially affects the result or user intent.

## Organic social media
Unpaid publishing and community interaction on social platforms. This is distinct from paid social advertising even when the same account, creative, or content source is involved.

## PPC
Pay-per-click, commonly used as shorthand for paid digital advertising where clicks are a billing or optimization unit. In practice, paid-media platforms support multiple bidding/billing models, so PPC should not be assumed to describe every paid campaign exactly.

## Practitioner position
An explicitly documented approach adopted from accumulated practical experience or project judgment. It may agree or disagree with common industry practice and should be labeled rather than disguised as a formal standard.

## Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG)
A generation architecture in which a model is supplied with retrieved external information to help construct a response. RAG is a broad technical pattern, not evidence that every commercial answer engine exposes or uses the same retrieval pipeline.

## Structured data
Machine-readable data describing entities/content and their relationships, such as Schema.org data serialized as JSON-LD. It must remain consistent with truthful visible content. Structured data support is platform-specific; vocabulary availability does not prove ranking or generative-answer use.

## T.E.S.T.I.N.G.
The canonical SEObasic content/community philosophy defined in [`../content/testing-philosophy.md`](../content/testing-philosophy.md). Its letters and meanings must not be inferred from this glossary or replaced with another expansion.

## Vanity metric
A metric that may show activity or attention but does not by itself establish useful business or audience outcomes—for example raw impressions, follower counts, clicks, or AI citation counts when the actual goal is qualified leads, sales, trust, retention, accurate representation, or another result.
