# SEObasic Strategies

> **Role:** What we deliberately do  
> **Parent:** [`../README.md`](../README.md)  
> **Agent instructions:** [`AGENTS.md`](AGENTS.md)

Strategies are deliberate methods used to improve relevance, usefulness, discoverability, communication, and business outcomes while respecting SEObasic invariants and surface-specific mechanics.

## Current strategy families

- [`content/`](content/README.md) — canonical T.E.S.T.I.N.G., content philosophy/application, and customer problem-solution framing.
- [`answer-discovery/`](answer-discovery/README.md) — AEO/GEO and answer/generative discovery strategy.
- [`entity-relationships/`](entity-relationships/README.md) — entity relationships, internal linking, and graph-oriented content relationships.
- [`on-page/keyword-use.md`](on-page/keyword-use.md) — natural keyword/topic-language use across owned content.

## Intent and content-pattern taxonomy

SEObasic may later add explicit strategy material for intent families such as:

- informational;
- commercial investigation/comparison;
- transactional;
- navigational/brand;
- local;
- problem resolution.

It may also add reusable content patterns such as comparison, FAQ/answer, case study, proof, service page, and location page.

Do **not** create these directories merely to complete a diagram. Add them when substantive governed knowledge exists.

## Strategy boundary

A strategy answers:

> **What deliberate approach are we taking?**

It does not answer:

- what a platform guarantees;
- what a crawler can access;
- what a metric means;
- what evidence proves a causal mechanism;
- what must remain universally true.

Route those questions respectively to [`../surfaces/`](../surfaces/README.md), [`../measurement/`](../measurement/README.md), [`../evidence/`](../evidence/README.md), and [`../invariants/`](../invariants/README.md).

## Cross-surface use

A strategy may be adapted across owned web, local/maps, social, paid media, YouTube, and generative/answer surfaces. Reuse does not make the mechanics of those surfaces interchangeable.

## Rule

> **Choose strategy from the evaluated problem; do not infer platform mechanics from the strategy itself.**
