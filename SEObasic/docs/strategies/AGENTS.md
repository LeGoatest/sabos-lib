# SEObasic Strategy Agent Instructions

> **Status:** Binding for work under `SEObasic/docs/strategies/`  
> **Parent authority:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)  
> **Domain entrypoint:** [`README.md`](README.md)

Strategies are deliberate interventions, content methods, and discovery approaches. They are not platform guarantees or evidence classes.

Agents MUST:

- start from a defined evaluation problem where practical;
- preserve [`../invariants/`](../invariants/README.md);
- adapt strategies to the named [`../surfaces/`](../surfaces/README.md) rather than assuming mechanics transfer unchanged;
- preserve canonical practitioner philosophies and positions;
- consult [`../evidence/`](../evidence/README.md) for material factual/causal claims;
- define outcomes through [`../measurement/`](../measurement/README.md).

Agents MUST NOT:

- turn a strategy into a universal ranking factor;
- infer platform behavior from a content pattern;
- silently rewrite canonical T.E.S.T.I.N.G. wording;
- treat one successful implementation as universal causation;
- promote a strategy to an invariant without explicit contract work;
- create intent/pattern directories merely to fill the taxonomy.

## Cross-surface rule

A strategy may be reused across owned web, generative/answer systems, local/maps, social, paid media, and YouTube, but each surface retains its own mechanics, policies, audience, presentation, attribution, and measurement definitions.
