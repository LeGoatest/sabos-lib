# T.E.S.T.I.N.G. Philosophy

> **Status:** Canonical  
> **Authority:** The wording below is the authoritative T.E.S.T.I.N.G. philosophy for SEObasic and is preserved as supplied.

[T.E.S.T.I.N.G. METHOD]
—————————————————————
T (Talk about the drive behind the passion): Highlight the passion and motivation driving the creation of the content, and what inspires the creator to do what they do.

E (Engage the audience): Ask questions or spark conversations that take interest in the audience’s thoughts and experiences. Can experiment with encourage the audience to interact (e.g., polls, questions, or quizzes).

S (Share updates of success and failures): Be transparent with both achievements and setbacks to build authenticity. Include behind-the-scenes moments or stories about the challenges and achievements.

T (Take time out to talk about others): Design posts around celebrating individuals, partners, or other related content. (e.g., authors, other creatives, organizations)

I (Investigate new ideas publicly): Experiment with ideas, share them openly, and invite feedback from the community.

N (Network responsibly): Develop strategies to connect with relevant partners or businesses and build meaningful collaborations. Choose what will benefit the community first.

G (Gather content regularly): Collect and share new content consistently, including videos, photos, and community submissions.

## Interpretation rule

The wording above defines T.E.S.T.I.N.G. Do not replace its seven meanings with a different acronym expansion.

Industry-, project-, campaign-, or platform-specific examples may specialize how the philosophy is applied, but they must not redefine the philosophy itself.
