# SEObasic Social Media Agent Instructions

> **Scope:** `SEObasic/social-media/`  
> **Parent authority:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)

## Mission

Preserve channel-specific reality while applying SEObasic content/community principles to organic social publishing.

## Required discipline

Agents MUST:

- distinguish organic social behavior from paid campaign behavior;
- verify current platform mechanics when recommendations depend on changing features, formats, or policies;
- treat platform analytics as context-specific signals rather than universal measures of business value;
- preserve attribution, permission, disclosure, and truthful claims;
- maintain the distinction between reusable source material and platform-specific presentation;
- route durable first-party information back to the website or other canonical owned surface when appropriate.

Agents MUST NOT:

- redefine T.E.S.T.I.N.G. to fit a platform;
- manufacture engagement, testimonials, partnerships, customer stories, or community proof;
- assume a tactic that works on one social platform transfers unchanged to another;
- confuse organic publishing guidance with PPC/paid-media contracts;
- optimize solely for vanity metrics when the stated objective is trust, leads, calls, sales, community, or another outcome.

## Contracts

Stable cross-campaign rules SHOULD be formalized as contracts when silent drift would materially damage authenticity, measurement, disclosure, or conversion behavior.
