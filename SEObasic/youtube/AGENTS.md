# SEObasic YouTube Agent Instructions

> **Scope:** `SEObasic/youtube/`  
> **Parent authority:** [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md)

## Mission

Preserve the distinction between YouTube-specific discovery/retention mechanics and broader content/community strategy.

## Required discipline

Agents MUST:

- verify current YouTube guidance when recommendations depend on changing platform features or policies;
- distinguish search-driven discovery, browse/recommendation discovery, Shorts behavior, and external traffic when relevant;
- treat titles/thumbnails as packaging, not substitutes for useful content;
- distinguish platform analytics from business outcomes;
- preserve captions, disclosures, attribution, permissions, and truthful claims;
- account for video durability and cross-channel reuse without assuming one format fits every surface.

Agents MUST NOT:

- guarantee recommendations, rankings, views, retention, or channel growth;
- invent performance data, audience demographics, sponsorships, testimonials, or collaborations;
- redefine T.E.S.T.I.N.G. around YouTube features;
- copy short-form social tactics into long-form YouTube guidance without evaluating format differences;
- optimize packaging through misleading clickbait that breaks the content promise.

## Contracts

Stable YouTube obligations SHOULD become contracts when repeatable channel work depends on them, especially truthful packaging, caption/accessibility requirements, disclosure, series/navigation relationships, and analytics definitions.
