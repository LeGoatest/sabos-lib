# DBbasic Changelog

All notable changes to DBbasic are tracked here. The research record remains informative until rules are deliberately adopted.

## [Unreleased]

### Added

- Initial governed relational-database knowledge system for single websites, multiuser applications, and multitenant web platforms.
- Preserved full October 23, 2026 Deep Research synthesis, with a curated primary-source reference catalog.
- Practical design guidance for normalized models, keys and junction tables, indexing, transactions, triggers, tenants, migrations, backup/recovery and engine selection.
- Proposed schema-integrity contract and a review checklist, expressly not yet binding.
- Illustrative SQLite relationship schema with cross-tenant referential integrity and an audit-trigger example.
